# Vision

## Project mission

Create a calm, professional, trustworthy online presence for Kwalietijd.

The website should feel like meeting an experienced professional: knowledgeable, approachable, and reliable.

## Brand personality

The design should communicate:

* Trust
* Professionalism
* Calmness
* Warmth
* Experience
* Personal attention

Avoid:

* Aggressive marketing
* Corporate buzzwords
* Flashy animations
* Sales-heavy language

## Primary audience

People who have already heard about Kwalietijd through:

* Professional referrals
* Existing clients
* LinkedIn
* HR departments
* Managers
* Healthcare networks

These visitors are usually validating whether they can trust the person behind the website.

## Core message

Kwalietijd offers professional bedrijfsmaatschappelijk werk with a personal approach, combining experience in healthcare, absenteeism management, coaching, and leadership.

## Success criteria

A visitor should be able to answer these questions within 10 seconds:

1. Who is this?
2. What does she do?
3. Why should I trust her?
4. How can I contact her?

## Design principles

* Clean layouts
* Plenty of whitespace
* Clear typography
* Professional photography
* Limited color palette
* High readability

Every design decision should support trust rather than attract attention.

## Visual identity

The visual identity should communicate professionalism, warmth, and reliability.

The design should feel calm and approachable rather than corporate or commercial.

The existing logo, color palette, and photography are the foundation of the design language and should be used consistently throughout the website.

The overall visual style should emphasize:

* Spacious layouts
* High readability
* Subtle use of color
* Professional photography
* Minimal visual distractions

Animations and decorative effects should be used sparingly and only when they improve the user experience.
